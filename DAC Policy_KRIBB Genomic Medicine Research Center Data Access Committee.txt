DAC Policy Title
KRIBB Genomic Medicine Research Center Data Access Committee

DAC Policy
The KRIBB Genomic Medicine Research Center Data Access Committee (DAC) is responsible for overseeing the access and use of genomic data derived from the study titled "Whole-genome sequences of the Korean population." This study generated long-read whole-genome sequencing data on the PacBio Revio and Oxford Nanopore PromethION platforms, together with Hi-C data, from 19 Korean individuals in order to construct the Korean Pangenome Reference (KPR). Access to these data is restricted to researchers conducting non-commercial, academic studies in health-related, biomedical or population-genomic research, including the development and benchmarking of analytical methods, consistent with the informed consent given by the research participants and with the approvals of the Institutional Review Boards of Chungnam National University Hospital (CNUH 2019-06-034) and Chungbuk National University Hospital. Applicants must submit a detailed research proposal, institutional ethics approval (IRB), and evidence of relevant expertise. All data accessed under this policy must be stored in secure environments, must not be shared with unauthorized third parties, and must not be used to identify individual research participants or their relatives, nor for forensic, law-enforcement, insurance, employment or marketing purposes. The data are provided strictly for the approved research project, and any further use requires additional DAC approval. Results derived from the use of these data must acknowledge the source study and cite the EGA study and dataset accession numbers. For inquiries or to request access, please contact Jae-Yoon Kim at jaeyoonkim@kribb.re.kr.
