# KPR access-policy implementation checklist

Operational checklist for the DAC, not a new applicant requirement. Proposed revision prepared on 26 November 2026. Items below require confirmation or completion; publication of this repository does not establish that these actions have occurred.

- [ ] Confirm the revised scope, informed-consent coverage (including assemblies from the three earlier Sequel II participants), eligibility criteria, five-member DAC composition, voting/conflict-of-interest arrangements, response targets and one-year access/renewal rules with KRIBB.
- [ ] Have the institutional authority review and approve the DAA, including signing authority, security, expiry, deletion/retention and incident handling.
- [ ] Confirm graph distribution rights and conditions, including the HPRC inputs, and ensure that both GFA files are covered by the revised approval/DAA.
- [ ] Complete the FASTA submission under EGAS50000002074; insert the actual assembly EGAD throughout this repository and the manuscript/pipeline README. Verify the dataset page, DAC and policy linkage. Do not publish a fabricated accession.
- [ ] Update the EGA policy text and any associated documentation to match the adopted policy, including removal of the subjective expertise criterion and the shortened moratorium; verify the existing policy's external URL in the DAC Portal and retain the stable GitHub policy PDF URL where applicable. Ensure that the new assembly dataset is linked to a suitable policy under EGAC50000001142.
- [ ] Implement the revised KGR download form and instructions: current assembly approval verification, name/affiliation/EGA email, optional internal DAC reference, no new research-purpose field, two-working-day target, seven-day maximum link validity, expiration/revocation handling, technical contact. Test valid, expired and revoked approvals, link forwarding risk and the link renewal process.
- [ ] Confirm the original KPR data release date and record the earlier of the first publication date of the primary KPR study and the 12-month deadline as the moratorium end date. Do not restart the embargo for later deposits.
- [ ] At the moratorium end date, manually update the relevant EGA Policy object(s) and remove DUO:0000024; update the public policy/DAA/checklist as applicable, and notify existing approved users that no further publication permission is required. Keep the source-study and relevant accession citation obligations.
- [ ] Once the revised policy is adopted and the EGA/KGR procedures are aligned, update the draft-status statements in README, policy, DAA and checklist. Record the effective date and version in both the Markdown and PDF documents; preserve the stable policy PDF filename and the archived original at `archive/KPR_DAC_Policy_1_original.pdf`.
- [ ] Respond to the outstanding journal data-curator access request and verify that access works.
- [ ] Update the main KPR-pipeline README, the manuscript's Data Description and Availability of supporting data, and the response to Reviewer 1 Comment 6 only with verified completed changes.

Official guidance: [EGA DAC Portal](https://ega-archive.org/access/data-access-committee/dac-portal/), [EGA policy documentation](https://ega-archive.org/access/data-access-committee/policy-documentation/).
