# KRIBB Korean Pangenome Project Data Access Application Checklist

*Version 1.0 — Effective 26 November 2026. The assembly dataset accession and revised KGR graph download procedure are pending availability.*

Review the [Data Access Policy](KPR_DAC_Policy.md) and prepare the Data Access Agreement ([Word](KPR_Data_Access_Agreement.docx), [PDF](KPR_Data_Access_Agreement.pdf)). All items below are required unless identified as conditional or optional. There is no scientific merit, novelty or research expertise assessment.

## Before submitting

- [ ] Confirm a defined, non-commercial project at a not-for-profit research institution and compatibility with participant consent, ethics and the policy.
- [ ] Select the resource(s) required: raw reads (EGAD50000002977), assemblies (new EGAD pending), and optionally KPR / HPRC+KPR graphs. For GFA access, request approval covering the KOR assemblies; downloading FASTA is not required.
- [ ] Each intended EGA user has an individual EGA account. Submit Request Access for the relevant dataset(s) from the EGA dataset page.

## Supporting documents

- [ ] Detailed research proposal: project title, objectives, planned use, duration, requested datasets/resources, and whether any derived individual-level resource will be distributed.
- [ ] Applicant institution (legal name, postal address, website and contact email) and principal investigator contact details.
- [ ] Named users and institutional affiliations, email addresses (EGA account emails where applicable), project roles and identification of users who need to download data from EGA.
- [ ] Documentation of institutional ethics approval for the proposed work, with reference number, issuing body and validity.
- [ ] Secure environment description: storage and analysis locations, access controls and authentication, secure transfer/storage (including encryption of portable storage), backup, incident response and data retention/deletion.
- [ ] Completed DAA signed by an authorized representative of the applicant institution, the principal investigator and all named users.

Send supporting materials privately to **jaeyoonkim@kribb.re.kr** with the EGA dataset accession(s) and your project title. Do not upload completed forms, personal details or approval documentation to public GitHub issues or pull requests. The blank DAA and this checklist are publicly available so no separate enquiry is needed to obtain the forms.

## Decision and provision of data

| Step | Target |
| --- | --- |
| Acknowledgement | 5 working days |
| Decision after all required materials are received | 10 working days |
| EGA access after approval and signed DAA | 5 working days |
| GFA link after verification of existing approval | 2 working days |

Working days are working days in the Republic of Korea. If additional information is needed, the DAC informs the applicant; the decision period restarts after the information is received. Refusals include written reasons; reconsideration may be requested within 30 days. Access is valid for one year, with renewal available under the policy.

## Graph download after approval

1. Go to the [KGR data download page](https://kgr.appex.kr/resource/data-download/) once the revised graph-request procedure is implemented.
2. Select the GFA file and enter your name, affiliation and EGA-registered email associated with the current assembly approval; provide a DAC approval reference only if one has been assigned (optional).
3. Confirm compliance with the DAA. No new research proposal or additional scientific review is required.
4. The DAC/KGR staff verify current access permissions and send the download link to the approved email within two working days. The link is valid for up to seven days, or until approval expires or is revoked, whichever occurs earlier. It may be re-requested during the approved period.

For access enquiries: jaeyoonkim@kribb.re.kr. For graph download technical issues: durud103@kribb.re.kr.
