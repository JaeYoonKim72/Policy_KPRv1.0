## B. Terms and conditions

1. **Authorized use.** The applicant institution and named users agree to use the approved resources only for the specified non-commercial research project at a not-for-profit research institution, within participant consent, applicable law and the documented institutional ethics approval. Access is limited to the resources, users and period approved in writing by the DAC. Changes in project scope, users, ethics approval or security environment require prior DAC approval or an approved amendment.
2. **Privacy and prohibited use.** No attempt may be made to identify or contact research participants or their relatives. Commercial, forensic, law-enforcement, insurance, employment and marketing uses are prohibited.
3. **Security and confidentiality.** The applicant institution and named users will restrict access to approved personnel, use individual credentials, appropriate authentication and access controls, and secure data transfer, storage and analysis. Credentials and private download links must not be shared. Controlled data and individual-level derivatives must not be posted to public repositories or disclosed to unauthorized parties. The institution will promptly inform the DAC of any actual or suspected unauthorized access, loss, disclosure or other security incident and cooperate with remediation.
4. **Derived resources.** Integration into broader resources is allowed within the approved project. Redistribution of resources containing individual-level KOR genomic data requires prior DAC approval and equivalent controlled-access terms. Aggregate, non-identifying research results may be shared within the approved scope and applicable ethical requirements, subject to clause 5.
5. **Publication and acknowledgement.** The publication moratorium ends upon the first publication of the primary KPR study or 12 months after the original KPR data were first made available, whichever occurs earlier; subsequent deposits do not restart it. Before that date, manuscripts based on the data may be submitted with written KRIBB consent. After that date, no further publication-related approval or embargo applies to research within the approved scope. Users must acknowledge the source study and cite the EGA study and relevant dataset accession numbers.
6. **Graph access.** Users with current DAC approval covering the KOR assemblies may request KPR and HPRC+KPR GFA files through the KGR download page. Verification of identity and current permissions is not another scientific review. Graphs remain subject to this agreement. Download links are valid for up to seven days, or until approval expires or is revoked, whichever occurs earlier, and must not be forwarded.
7. **Duration, renewal and termination.** Approval is valid for one year from the DAC's grant date, unless withdrawn earlier. Renewal requires written confirmation that the project, named users, ethics approval and secure environment remain unchanged and valid. On expiry without renewal, project completion, or revocation, users must cease use and securely delete or return controlled data and individual-level derivatives, including copies, in accordance with applicable institutional retention obligations and any written DAC instructions. Where legally required retention prevents immediate deletion, retained copies must remain securely restricted and unavailable for further research use, and the DAC must be informed. Non-identifying aggregate results may be retained. The institution must confirm compliance to the DAC upon request.
8. **Compliance and suspension.** The institution is responsible for ensuring that every named user complies with this agreement. The DAC may suspend or withdraw access for non-compliance or where continued access is inconsistent with participant consent, ethics approval or applicable requirements. The DAC will communicate the reasons in writing and provide a route to reconsideration under the policy.
9. **No transfer of access rights.** Permission to use data does not confer ownership or authorize onward access. Each new user must be approved before gaining access. The obligations concerning confidentiality, privacy, secure retention and prohibited redistribution continue after the access period ends.
